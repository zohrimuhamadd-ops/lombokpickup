About this tour

This is the classic day out in South Lombok, and it is private: one car, one driver, one price for up to six people. You visit Selong Belanak, Lancing and Mawun, stop for lunch at a restaurant you choose, swim at Tanjung Aan and finish on Merese Hill for the sunset.

Because the car is yours for the day, you can stay longer on the beach you like and skip the one you do not. Your driver knows the road and the best time to reach each beach.

Pick-up is from your hotel or villa in Kuta, Mandalika, Selong Belanak, Senggigi or Mataram, or from Lombok Airport. Tell us where you stay and we confirm the pick-up time on WhatsApp.

Where you go: Selong Belanak, Lancing Beach, Mawun Beach, Lunch at a restaurant of your choice, Tanjung Aan, Merese Hill at sunset.

Full day, about 9.5 hours. You choose your pick-up time (flyer plan: 09:00, back after sunset around 18:30).

Questions about this tour

Yes. You have your own car and driver for the whole day. There are no other guests unless you bring them.

Yes. Because the tour is private, you choose your pick-up time when you book. We confirm it with you on WhatsApp.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before the tour.

How and when do I pay?

You pay your driver on the day. You do not pay anything to send a booking request.

What is included?

Private car pick-up; Fuel during the trip; Driver as tour guide; Parking and entrance fees; Drop-off after sunset.
